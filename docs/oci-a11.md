# OCI direct blob origin (A11)

The Worker can answer blob GET and HEAD with a `307` to a separate blob host. This records the deployed comparison that decided whether to keep that path.

- Local proof: `deploy/test-oci-redirect.sh` starts the Worker front and a separate blob host, and Docker follows the Worker's `307` to the blob host.
- O2 gate: compare the deployed matrix with and without `STUFF_OCI_BLOB_ORIGIN`. Adopt only if warm p95 and full-pull wall time improve by at least 10% with non-overlapping p95 over five interleaved runs.
- Method: `myoci bench --runs 1` against `https://oci.17166969.xyz` repository `example/bench` (1 MiB, 64 MiB, 256 MiB, and 1 GiB layers), five rounds alternating the order of variant A (Worker path, origin unset) and variant B (origin set to `https://oci-blobs.17166969.xyz`, Cache Rule on `/sha256/*`). The redirect was toggled through the Worker secret and confirmed by five consecutive probes before each run. A run was discarded when `perf-gate-ready` reported load afterwards; discarded and failed-to-toggle runs were repeated. Client: Darwin 27.0.0 arm64 over one residential connection, 2026-11-01. Five samples per cell, so p95 is the slowest sample.
- Result, warm milliseconds per sample set, A then B:

| Row | A p50 | A p95 | B p50 | B p95 | p50 change | p95 change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 MiB warm | 138 | 484 | 242 | 270 | -76% | +44% |
| 64 MiB warm | 1277 | 5649 | 1168 | 1998 | +9% | +65% |
| 256 MiB warm | 5041 | 6559 | 3963 | 4130 | +21% | +37% |
| 1024 MiB warm | 29750 | 50269 | 15729 | 25263 | +47% | +50% |
| first pull | 37716 | 55492 | 22317 | 26329 | +41% | +53% |
| repeat pull | 35619 | 54058 | 21669 | 25108 | +39% | +54% |

- Decision: adopted. Measured on p95, as the gate is written, every row improves by at least 37%. On medians the 64 MiB row misses 10% and 1 MiB blobs are about 100 ms slower, the cost of connecting to a second host; the full pulls and the 256 MiB and 1 GiB layers improve by 21% or more. The 1 GiB layer exceeds the Worker's 512 MB cacheable-object cap on the Worker path, which accounts for part of that row. Caveats: one client location, five samples, and a shared host with one discarded run.
- Status: `STUFF_OCI_BLOB_ORIGIN` is set in `cloudflare.config.ts`.

The loopback matrix in [oci-benchmarks.md](oci-benchmarks.md) is regenerated by `deploy/oci-bench.sh` and does not answer this question.
